# Using the iOS app

## Temperature

Choose left or right, then drag the radial dial or use the step controls. Link mode lets you apply changes to both sides. The app distinguishes current temperature from the requested target; the glow communicates heating or cooling.

## Schedule

Choose a side and day, adjust the temperature curve by phase, and set bedtime, alarm, and power controls. Cool, Balanced, and Warm presets provide starting points. Review the final values before saving.

## Sleep

The Sleep tab shows one night, a week or a month. A night has the sleep summary, a stage timeline (awake, REM, light, deep) and the night's heart rate, HRV and breathing rate; tap a vital for its full chart. Stages are estimated on the iPhone from the Pod's vitals, so gaps in source data affect what can be shown.

### Apple Health and Apple Watch

Settings → Apple Health turns on a morning sync: last night's stages, heart rate, HRV and respiratory rate are written to Health for this iPhone's side of the bed, and the night view shows when it was saved. With read access to Sleep, Heart Rate, HRV and Respiratory Rate, any night gains a **Compare with Apple Watch** page: the Pod's stages above the Watch's, the percentage of minutes that agree, where they disagree, heart rate as two lines with the average error, and HRV and breathing as night averages. The Week view tracks agreement per night. Only the side whose sleeper wears the Watch is compared.

## Sensors and status

The sensor view provides live measurements. Status groups service health, Wi-Fi, water level, calibration, model inventory, and logs. Refresh if a value appears stale.

Some status actions affect the running Pod, including calibration and internet-access settings. Check the selected device and read the action's prompt before changing operational settings.

## Troubleshooting the app

- **No devices:** verify Local Network permission, network isolation, and manual IP connectivity.
- **Controls fail:** confirm Core is running and the backend choice matches your server.
- **Charts are empty:** check the selected side/date and the biometrics services in Core.
- **An update breaks a screen:** include app/Core versions and the affected action in an [iOS issue](https://github.com/sleepypod/ios/issues).

## Newer build controls

The iOS development build identified as 1.1.1 adds the following controls. The screenshots above retain their separately pinned redesign revision; the exact layout depends on your installed build.

- **3D thermal bed:** Sensors shows a bed surface view built from measured temperatures, alongside the temperature trend. It visualizes readings; it does not add independently controlled heating zones.
- **Sweeping piezo trace:** the cursor writes new incoming samples over the previous sweep, keeping the remaining trace still.
- **After schedule ends:** when the connected backend supports it, the schedule editor offers **Turn off** or **Maintain final temperature**. Maintaining keeps the final target until a manual change, schedule, or automation takes over; auto-off limits still apply. Device settings can set the default for new curves without changing existing schedules.

## Development preview: Health rebuild and Watch charts

These additions are pinned to iOS development revision `ce1d455`, compared with `main` at `bb81c0a` on October 14, 2026. They are not yet on `main`; availability depends on your installed build. The screenshots earlier on this page show an older revision.

### Sync or rebuild Apple Health

**Settings → Apple Health → Sync last 7 nights now** retries recent nights for this iPhone’s selected side. The sync uses full nights and rewrites a night when its analysis changes, including changed stage rules. Check the side and any reported failures in the sync status.

**Rebuild last 90 nights** is a broader action. After confirmation, it rewrites the last 90 nights from the Pod using the current analysis, then deletes other data that sleepypod previously wrote to Health, including older nights. Apple Watch and other apps’ data are not touched. Keep the app open until it finishes. If rewriting fails, cleanup of older data is skipped; fix the reported error and retry. Demo mode never writes to Health.

### Read the Watch comparison

From a night in Sleep, open **Compare with Apple Watch**. The newer comparison adds heart-rate, HRV, and breathing charts you can touch to read at a specific time, a stage overlay showing disagreement, and a table of minutes classified as each pair of stages. HRV values can represent different metrics between the Pod and Watch; differences alone do not establish which device is more accurate. A later development revision adds **Compare stage models** and a **Pod model** choice under Settings; see [Personal stage models](/core/personal-stage-models/).

The Watch’s night no longer has to fit entirely inside the bed’s recorded window. The app selects the Watch session with the largest overlap; if none overlaps, it can select the nearest session within 12 hours. Check both time spans before interpreting the comparison. Stage agreement uses the overlapping classified epochs, and heart-rate pairing requires readings close in time. Missing Watch data remains missing rather than counting as agreement.
