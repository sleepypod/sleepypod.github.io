## Made for everyday control

Use the radial dial, select a side or link both, and review your night through interactive charts. The app can discover Core automatically with Bonjour or connect using a manual IP address.

## Requirements and availability

The repository specifies **iOS 26.0+, Xcode 26+, and Swift 6.0**. The documented installation route is a source build with Xcode. This site does not assume an App Store or TestFlight release.

[Build and connect →](/ios/setup/)

## What runs where

Core controls the hardware and stores server-side schedules and sensor data. iOS is the client and performs on-device sleep-stage analysis. Keep Core running on the Pod even when the app is closed.
