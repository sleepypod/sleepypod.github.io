## Made for everyday control

Use the dial, slider or Night & Dawn card, select a side or link both, and review your night through interactive charts. Each morning the night's stages, heart rate, HRV and breathing rate can be written to Apple Health, and any night can be compared minute by minute with what an Apple Watch recorded. The app can discover Core automatically with Bonjour or connect using a manual IP address.

## Requirements and availability

The repository specifies **iOS 26.0+, Xcode 26+, and Swift 6.0**. The documented installation route is a source build with Xcode; a TestFlight beta is in progress.

[Build and connect →](/ios/setup/)

## What runs where

Core controls the hardware and stores server-side schedules and sensor data. iOS is the client and performs on-device sleep-stage analysis. Keep Core running on the Pod even when the app is closed.
