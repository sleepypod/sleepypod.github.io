# Sensors

**System → Sensors** shows live readings from every sensor the Pod has. Use it to check that data is arriving. Nothing here changes the Pod.

<BrowserFrame
  src="/media/core-system-sensors.png"
  alt="Sensors page with presence, the bed temperature matrix, freezer health, a six-hour bed temperature chart, humidity, the piezo waveform, and flow rate"
  caption="System → Sensors with synthetic live frames and history."
/>

| Card                | Shows                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Presence**        | Whether each side is in bed, moving or still, and activity by zone: head, torso, and legs                                                    |
| **Bed temp matrix** | Outer, centre, and inner surface temperature for each side, plus room (ambient), board (mcu), and humidity                                   |
| **Freezer health**  | Status (**Normal**, **Water low**, **High TEC**, **Fan slow**, or **No data**), heatsink and water temperature, and pump speed for each side |
| **Bed temperature** | Left, Right, and Ambient over **1h**, **6h**, or **24h**                                                                                     |
| **Humidity**        | Room humidity with min, average, and max                                                                                                     |
| **Piezo**           | The raw heartbeat and breathing waveform. Seek back in time, then **Go live** to return                                                      |
| **Flow rate**       | Water flow or pump **RPM** for each side, over the time you choose                                                                           |

Each card shows how old its reading is. A missing card value (`--`) means no reading, not zero. Which cards have data depends on the Pod generation and firmware.

If readings are missing, check the live stream bar at the top, then [Health](/core/system/health/).
