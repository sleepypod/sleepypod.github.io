# Thermal

**System → Thermal** shows whether the Pod is actually delivering the temperature you asked for. Use it when a side does not feel right. Nothing here changes the Pod.

## Read the charts

Choose a range: **Live**, **1 h**, **12 h** (default), **24 h**, or **7 d**. Hover to read exact values.

- **Bed and target**: the bed temperature for each side, with its target as a dashed line.
- **Water**: the water temperature leaving each side's heat pump.
- **Surface**: the cover's surface sensors.
- **Pump rpm**: how fast each side's pump spins.
- **Hub**: heatsink and room temperature inside the Pod.

Marks show when a side was turned on. The line at the top shows the current heatsink and hub temperature.

## Side cards

Each side shows **Target**, **Bed**, **Pump · rpm**, **Water**, **Surface**, and how fresh the flow reading is, with **WARMING**, **COOLING**, or **HOLDING**. Badges flag **GUARD BLOCKED** (pump protection turned the side off) and **ALARM VIBRATING**.

## A side is not heating or cooling

1. Check the target is what you expect, and the side is on.
2. Compare **Water** with **Bed**. Water should be warmer than the bed when warming and cooler when cooling.
3. Check **Pump rpm**. A pump near zero is not circulating. Missing pump data is not the same as zero; some firmware does not report it.
4. Check the water level on the [Dashboard](/core/system/#attention-card), and **Pump alerts**.

If **Pump-stall protection off** is shown, consider turning it on in [Device](/core/settings/device/#pump-protection).
