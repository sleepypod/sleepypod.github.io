# Logs

**System → Logs** shows recent messages from the Pod's services. Read it when something failed and you want to know why. Nothing here changes the Pod.

## Read a service's log

1. Under **Sources**, choose a service: **Core**, **Piezo Processor**, **Sleep Detector**, or **Environment Monitor**. A green dot means it is running.
2. Filter by level with **All**, **Info**, **Warn**, or **Error**, or type in **Filter** to find a word.
3. Leave **Follow** on to keep the newest lines in view as they arrive. Turn it off to scroll back.

The viewer shows the last 200 lines. **Copy** and **Download** save them for a bug report. Select the refresh button to reload.

The Health page's **Logs** buttons open this page with the right service already chosen.

## Firmware console

**Open console** shows raw output from the Pod's firmware. Switch between **Logs** and **Frames**, filter by type, **Pause** or **Resume**, and **Clear** the view. Use it to check the firmware is sending data at all.
