# Temp

**Temp** is the home screen. It shows both sides of the bed, and lets you change temperature and power right now. Choose the correct side before changing anything.

The Stage-default layout below is from [Core revision `2c28d2e5`](https://github.com/sleepypod/core/commit/2c28d2e5) on `dev`. Released v3.2.1 starts in Cards and still has a thermal card on Temp.

## Explore the temperature Stage

Temp opens in **Stage**, a full-screen 3D bed. The **Cards | Stage** switch changes the layout and saves your choice in this browser.

<BrowserFrame
  src="/media/core-stage.png"
  alt="Temperature Stage with the 3D bed, per-side targets, surface sensor readings and a schedule timeline"
  caption="The temperature Stage. Real application rendering with simulated sensor data."
/>

Click either side of the bed to bring it into focus and slide its controls into view. The Stage uses the same temperature controls and manual holds as the cards. Dragging on a bed side adjusts its target and turns it on if it was off. Linking sides copies the left target and power to the right, then applies subsequent changes to both.

The timeline lets you **preview** scheduled temperatures. Scrubbing changes the visualization, not the Pod; choose **Back to live** to leave the preview. The live bed colours represent measured surface temperature, while side labels show targets when on. See the [schedule-preview screenshot](/media/core-stage-preview.png). The six zones are sensor readings, not six independently controlled heating zones. Missing or stale readings are shown as unknown rather than invented temperatures.

On a keyboard, **1 / 2** selects a side, **↑ / ↓** adjusts it, **L** links the sides, **Space** changes power, and **Esc** backs out of a preview or selection. The side controls also remain available as ordinary buttons and text.

## Read the thermal bed

<BrowserFrame
  src="/media/core-thermal-bed.png"
  alt="System Sensors showing the 3D thermal bed and six measured temperature regions"
  caption="Thermal view in the real app, with simulated temperatures."
/>

Find the 3D thermal bed in **[System → Sensors](/core/system/sensors/)**, under **Bed temp matrix**. It shows outer, centre, and inner surface readings on each side; it is no longer a card on Temp. A target is what you asked for; a surface reading is what the sensor currently measures. Open [System → Thermal](/core/system/thermal/) for the longer diagnostic trends.

<ControlShowcase compact />

With **Dial** or **Slider**, drag to preview and let go to send. **Cooler** and **Warmer** (− and +) change it one degree at a time; quick presses are sent together. The range is 55–110°F. The word under the number says **COOLING**, **WARMING**, or **HOLDING**, and the bed's measured temperature is shown below. The target and the bed often differ while the bed is heating or cooling.

## Now, Night, and Dawn

The stepper separates a change for right now from a change to your nightly schedule:

| Control   | What changes                                         | What to check                                             |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Now**   | The side's current target, with a manual hold        | The hold length and power                                 |
| **Night** | Saves an edit to the earlier part of tonight's curve | The days shown: every day with the same curve changes too |
| **Dawn**  | Saves an edit to the last part of that curve         | The time range and matching days                          |

**Night** and **Dawn** shift that part of the curve together, keeping its shape. A one-point schedule has no Dawn.

If the side has no schedule tonight, the stepper shows **Set up Night & Dawn** and suggested values from a balanced 10 PM–7 AM curve. Your first − or + saves that curve to every night without a schedule. Check the result in [Schedule](/core/schedules/).

## Manual holds and Resume

Changing **Now**, the Dial, the Slider, or Cooler and Warmer starts a **manual hold**: a temporary change that wins over the schedule and Autopilot while it lasts.

Changing **Night** or **Dawn** is different. It saves a permanent edit to your schedule, for every day that shares tonight's curve, and does not start a hold. Undo it with the opposite change, or in [Schedule](/core/schedules/).

- **Hold after adjustment** sets how long: 15 min, **30 min** (default), 1 hr, or 2 hr. Each change restarts the timer.
- The line under the side's name shows who is in control: **On schedule**, **Autopilot**, **Run-once session**, **Manual hold · until 11:30 PM**, **Off**, or **Safety stop**.
- **Resume** ends a hold early.

When a hold ends, the side goes to whatever applies **now**. It does not replay schedule changes it missed. Your schedule stays switched on throughout. For the full rules, see [who controls the temperature](/core/autopilot-engine/#who-controls-the-temperature).

## Power

- The round button on each side card turns that side on or off.
- **All off** turns off every side that is on.
- **Link sides** (on a computer) makes every change apply to both sides until you select **Sides linked** again. On a phone, use the link icon between the side buttons.

With the standard cards, a temperature change does not turn an off side on. In Stage, dragging directly on a side can turn it on as described above. If a side says it is on but the bed is not changing temperature, check [System → Thermal](/core/system/thermal/) rather than changing the target again.

## The rest of the Cards layout

- **Autopilot chip**: shows **AUTOPILOT · N ACTIVE**, **DRY RUN**, or **HALTED** when you have rules. Select it to open [Autopilot](/core/autopilot/).
- **Room**: room temperature, humidity, the bed's air temperature, and light level.
- **Last night**: sleep length, bed and wake times, exits, and average heart rate, HRV, and breathing. Select it to open [Sleep](/core/biometrics/).
- **Alarm**: the next alarm, with a switch to turn that alarm on or off. Select it to edit, or **Add** if there is none.
- **Schedule and sleep** (on a computer): last night and tonight for each side, with the schedule, when the side was on, and when someone was in bed. Notes such as **bed empty while cooling** point out wasted heating or cooling.
- **Tonight** (on a phone): tonight's window and the next change.

## Banners

Banners appear at the top when something needs attention:

- **Pump stall**: a side was turned off because its pump stalled. **Re-enable** turns it back on at its previous temperature. Dismissing leaves it off. See [pump protection](/core/settings/device/#pump-protection).
- **Priming** and **Priming complete**.
- **Alarm active**: **Snooze 5m** or **Stop**. While snoozed, **Cancel** stops the alarm.
