# HomeKit

**Settings → HomeKit** adds the Pod to Apple Home. Pairing and control stay on your local network; no Apple servers are involved.

## Pair with Apple Home

1. Turn on **HomeKit bridge**. The status changes from **Starting** to **Running**.
2. A **Setup code** and QR code appear.
3. On your iPhone, open the **Home** app, choose **Add Accessory**, and scan the QR code or enter the eight-digit code.
4. Assign the new accessories to a room.

**Paired homes** shows how many Apple homes are paired. Until you pair, it says **None yet**.

Core adds a thermostat, power switch, occupancy sensor, and snooze switch for each side, plus priming and room-temperature accessories for the Pod. See the [HomeKit reference](/core/integrations/#apple-home--homekit) for how each one behaves.

## Reset pairing

**Reset HomeKit pairing** appears once a home is paired. It gives the bridge a new identity, with a new code and QR.

<Callout type="warning">
  After a reset, the old bridge stays in the Home app and its tiles show **No Response**. Remove it
  from Home yourself, then pair again with the new code.
</Callout>

Keep the setup code private. Anyone on your network with the code can pair while the bridge is waiting for a home.
