# Sides

**Settings → Sides** holds each person's preferences. Choose **Left** or **Right** at the top of the page first. Each change saves straight away.

## Bed setup

Choose **Two sleepers**, **Solo · left**, or **Solo · right** for who normally sleeps here. This is separate from a partner's temporary Away mode and from temperature linking.

**Other temperature zone** decides what happens when one sleeper is home:

- **Stay off** keeps the unused zone off.
- **Follow my schedule** follows the active sleeper's schedule.
- **Use its own schedule** keeps the other zone's saved schedule.

Both zones retain their saved schedules. An absent sleeper's alarms are paused. One active sleeper gets one sleep record using sensors across the bed; manual controls remain available for both temperature zones.

## Profile

**Name** is shown throughout the app, for example on the Temp screen and in the Person switches. Use up to 20 characters. It saves when you press Enter or leave the field. **Side** shows which side of the bed this is and cannot be changed.

## Away mode

Turn on **Away** while this person is away. It preserves the normal bed setup and pauses that person's alarms. The **Other temperature zone** choice determines whether their zone stays off, follows the active sleeper, or runs its own schedule. Turn Away off when they return.

## Auto-off

**Auto-off** turns the side off after the person gets out of bed. Choose how long to wait in **Turn off after**, from 5 minutes to 2 hours (30 minutes by default), and turn on the switch.

Auto-off needs to know when someone is in bed. The badge shows **Presence available** when it does. If it shows **Presence unavailable**, you cannot turn Auto-off on. Calibrate the capacitance sensor for this side in [System → Calibration](/core/system/calibration/), then come back.

## Always on

The Pod's firmware normally turns a side off after 8 hours. **Always on** prevents that. Always-on sides are also exempt from the [auto power-off cap](/core/settings/device/#power).

Always on and Auto-off cannot both be on. Turning one on turns the other off.
