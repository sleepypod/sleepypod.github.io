# Device

**Settings → Device** holds settings for the whole Pod. Every control saves as soon as you change it, and a **Saved** message confirms it.

## Clock and schedule defaults

**Time format** chooses **12-hour** or **24-hour** display. This is saved on the Pod, so connected clients share the preference; it does not change the timezone or the times stored in your schedules.

**Default after schedule ends** chooses **Turn off** or **Maintain final temperature** for new curves. Existing curves retain their own choice. See [schedule end actions](/core/schedules/#what-happens-at-the-end).

## Pod

- **Timezone.** Schedules, alarms, and Autopilot time windows run in this timezone. If things happen at the wrong hour, check this first. The default is America/Los_Angeles.
- **Temperature unit.** °F or °C for the whole Pod. This is the same setting as **Units** in [Appearance](/core/settings/appearance/).

## Power

**Auto power-off cap** turns a side off after it has been on for a set time. Turn on the switch and choose 1 to 48 hours; the default is 12 hours. Sides set to **Always on** in [Sides](/core/settings/sides/), and run-once sessions, are not affected.

## Pump protection

**Pump stall protection** turns a side off if its water pump slows down or stops, which protects the Pod when water is not flowing. It is off until you turn it on. While it is off, the [System dashboard](/core/system/) shows a reminder.

When it is on, you can adjust:

| Setting                | Range        | Default | Meaning                                                               |
| ---------------------- | ------------ | ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Trip threshold**     | 100–1500 RPM | 500     | Pump speed below which a reading counts as stalled                    |
| **Dwell samples**      | 1–10         | 2       | Stalled readings in a row, about 60 seconds apart, before turning off |
| **Pump auto-recovery** | On/off       | Off     | Turn the side back on once the pump recovers                          |
| **Recovery RPM**       | 500–3000 RPM | 1500    | Pump speed that counts as recovered                                   |
| **Recovery samples**   | 1–10         | 3       | Recovered readings in a row before turning the side back on           |

Number fields save when you leave the field. Values outside the range are adjusted to the nearest allowed value.

When protection turns a side off, the Temp screen shows a banner. **Re-enable** turns the side back on at its previous temperature. Stall events also appear in **Pump alerts** on the [System dashboard](/core/system/).

## Reconnect and Restart service

- **Reconnect** reloads the page. Use it if the app stops updating.
- **Restart service** asks for confirmation, then makes the Pod briefly unavailable while Core restarts.

<Callout type="warning">
  In Core v3.0, **Restart service** runs the updater for the **main** channel, the same as **Check
  for updates** in [Updates](/core/settings/updates/). If you use the **dev** channel, it switches
  the Pod to main. Use it only when you are happy to update.
</Callout>

## Sensor data source

This card is read-only. It tells you which firmware pipeline the Pod is on and whether sensor frames are reaching Core, which is the first thing to check when biometrics are empty.

- **Firmware.** How the Pod's firmware hands sensor data to Core. Newer firmware (April 2026 and later) publishes to a local **NATS JetStream** server. Older firmware writes **.RAW** files, either on a tmpfs through a `frank.sh` shim or a `frank.service` drop-in, or straight to `/persistent`.
- **Reading from.** The source Core chose when the service started: **NATS stream** or **.RAW files**. Core detects this itself, once per start, so after a firmware update it needs a restart to detect again. **Check for updates** in [Updates](/core/settings/updates/) rebuilds and restarts Core on your current channel. **Restart service** on this page also restarts it, but in Core v3.0 it runs the **main** channel updater, so skip it on **dev** (see the warning under Reconnect and Restart service).
- **Last frame.** How long ago the newest sensor frame arrived, and its type. **Live** means frames are flowing. **Stalled** means nothing has arrived for over a minute.

A note appears under the rows when the chosen source does not match the firmware, or when an environment override (`PIEZO_SENSOR_SOURCE` or `PIEZO_NATS_DISABLED`) is forcing a source. On a development machine without a Pod the card shows no firmware.

<Callout type="info">
  There is no switch here on purpose. Core picks one source per start and never runs both at once,
  so a toggle would need a restart to take effect. The same information is in the [System
  dashboard](/core/system/) under **Health**, and on the Pod in `sp-status`.
</Callout>

## Daily maintenance

- **Daily reboot.** Restarts the Pod at the time you choose. Off by default; the default time is 03:00.
- **Daily prime.** Circulates water to clear air from the lines at the time you choose. Off by default; the default time is 14:00. Choose a time when nobody is in bed.

## LED

- **Brightness.** 0–100% for the Pod's light. It saves when you let go of the slider.
- **Night mode.** Dims the light during a time window. Choose the **Window** (22:00–07:00 by default) and the **Night brightness** (0% by default).
