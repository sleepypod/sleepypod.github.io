# A tour of the new interfaces

A quick look around sleepypod Core: a new view of your bed, more control over your night, and the data behind it.

The Stage-default interface shown here is from [Core revision `2c28d2e5`](https://github.com/sleepypod/core/commit/2c28d2e5), currently on `dev`. The released v3.2.1 opens in Cards; choose Stage there to explore its 3D controls.

## Transcript and chapters

1. **Your bed. Your rules.** A new view of your night: temperature, schedules and sleep in one local interface.
2. **See your bed in a new light.** The [temperature Stage](/core/temperature/#explore-the-temperature-stage) opens by default. The video clicks each side to animate the camera and reveal its controls, then lets you preview tonight's schedule before returning to live control. Previewing does not change the Pod.
3. **Feel the whole picture.** [System → Sensors](/core/system/sensors/) is home to the thermal bed. Its six surface readings show what the bed measures, using a fixed temperature colour scale.
4. **Find your wind-down.** [Base](/core/base/) previews adjustable-bed positions. Both sides move together. This feature is experimental and the video shows simulated hardware, not a verified physical installation.
5. **A curve that fits your night.** In [Schedule](/core/schedules/), choose whether to turn off or maintain the final temperature when a curve ends. The video changes an unsaved example curve; select Save curve to apply your own edit.
6. **Know your night.** [Sleep](/core/biometrics/) brings nights and vitals together. These are example readings, not a diagnosis.
7. **Trace every signal.** [System → Health](/core/system/health/) follows the sensor data path. This demo includes a simulated DAC-monitor stall, showing how the map points to a failing service.
8. **Try it. Make it yours.** Explore the live demo and use these guides with your own Pod.

## More changes to explore

- [Solo bed setup and Away mode](/core/settings/sides/#bed-setup) are separate choices, with an explicit policy for the other temperature zone.
- [12-hour or 24-hour time](/core/settings/device/#clock-and-schedule-defaults) is saved on the Pod.
- [Alarm wake windows](/core/schedules/#vibration-alarms) can ring early on movement, or at the saved time if no movement is detected.
- The [live Sensors view](/core/system/sensors/) draws incoming piezo data as a sweeping trace.

The overview and feature clips have player controls and do not autoplay. Use the transcript above if you prefer to read. The [media workflow](/developers/media/) explains source pinning and capture provenance.
