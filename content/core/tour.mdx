## Transcript and chapters

1. **Your bed. Your rules.** A new view of your night: temperature, schedules and sleep in one local interface.
2. **See your bed in a new light.** The [temperature Stage](/core/temperature/#explore-the-temperature-stage) shows surface sensor readings and lets you preview tonight's schedule before returning to live control. Previewing does not change the Pod.
3. **Find your wind-down.** [Base](/core/base/) previews adjustable-bed positions. Both sides move together. This feature is experimental and the video shows simulated hardware, not a verified physical installation.
4. **A curve that fits your night.** In [Schedule](/core/schedules/), choose whether to turn off or maintain the final temperature when a curve ends. The video changes an unsaved example curve; select Save curve to apply your own edit.
5. **Know your night.** [Sleep](/core/biometrics/) brings nights and vitals together. These are example readings, not a diagnosis.
6. **Trace every signal.** [System → Health](/core/system/health/) follows the sensor data path. This demo includes a simulated DAC-monitor stall, showing how the map points to a failing service.
7. **Try it. Make it yours.** Explore the live demo and use these guides with your own Pod.

## More changes to explore

- [Solo bed setup and Away mode](/core/settings/sides/#bed-setup) are separate choices, with an explicit policy for the other temperature zone.
- [12-hour or 24-hour time](/core/settings/device/#clock-and-schedule-defaults) is saved on the Pod.
- [Alarm wake windows](/core/schedules/#vibration-alarms) can ring early on movement, or at the saved time if no movement is detected.
- The [live Sensors view](/core/system/sensors/) draws incoming piezo data as a sweeping trace.

The overview and feature clips have player controls and do not autoplay. Use the transcript above if you prefer to read. The [media workflow](/developers/media/) explains source pinning and capture provenance.
