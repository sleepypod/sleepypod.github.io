# Sleep

**Sleep** shows what the Pod measured while you slept. It has two views: **Nights** for sleep records and **Biometrics** for heart rate, HRV, and breathing. Everything is processed on the Pod; see [Follow your data](/core/data-flow/) for how.

Choose whose data you see with **Left** / **Right** at the top. The switch is hidden while one side is in [Away mode](/core/settings/sides/#away-mode).

## Nights

Choose **Night**, **Week**, or **Month**, and move between weeks or months with ‹ and ›.

### Night

- **The night card** shows a quality score, the date, and bed and wake times. Below it, the time spent **Awake**, in **REM**, **Light**, and **Deep** sleep, and a hypnogram of the stages through the night. With too little data, it says **Not enough vitals to classify stages for this night**.
- **Heart rate through the night**, with minimum, average, and maximum.
- **Sleeping HR**, **HRV**, and **Breathing** averages.
- **Summary**: Bedtime, Wake, Asleep, and Bed exits.
- **This week**: one bar per night. Select a bar to open that night.

**Edit times** corrects a night's bedtime and wake time, or deletes the night. Deleting cannot be undone.

### Export your data

**Export raw data**, at the bottom, shows how much data the Pod holds for the selected side and week, then lets you download:

- CSV files for vitals, sleep sessions, and movement, or **Export all as CSV**.
- **Export archive (.tar.gz)**: the raw sensor files for the selected week, plus a full copy of the sleep database. The database copy is not limited to the selected side or week: it holds **both sides' complete history**. Check before you share it.
- Individual raw sensor files, which you can also delete. The newest file cannot be deleted.

## Biometrics

**Sleep → Biometrics** shows vitals across a range: **Night**, **Week** (the last 7 days, default), or **Month** (the last 30 days).

<BrowserFrame
  src="/media/core-biometrics.png"
  alt="Biometrics view with a week of heart-rate, HRV, and breathing measurements"
  caption="Sleep → Biometrics. Real core UI with deterministic synthetic measurements, not personal data."
/>

- **Summary**: average heart rate, heart-rate range, average HRV, and average breathing.
- **Vitals chart**: the readings over the range, with a band for the middle half. Empty stretches are removed. Select a session to zoom; **clear** zooms out.
- **Sides**: whether each side is in bed, whether presence is available, movement, and level.
- **Sessions**: one row per night. Select one to zoom the chart to it.
- **Raw log**: every per-minute reading in a searchable table. **Raw data** opens the export described above.

### When readings are missing

The page warns you when something looks wrong:

- If a side has read **occupied** for 2 hours with no movement and no vitals, it asks whether someone is really in bed. **No — recalibrate empty bed** recalibrates the presence sensor. **Yes — restart piezo processor** restarts the vitals service.
- If someone is in bed but no vitals have arrived for 15 minutes, it suggests the processing may be stalled, with a **Health →** link to [System → Health](/core/system/health/).

Missing readings are missing, not zero. Sensor availability depends on the Pod generation, firmware, and calibration; see [System → Calibration](/core/system/calibration/).

## Understand the limits

The app also records a sleep session from power changes when sensor processing is not running, so a night can exist without vitals. Estimates are useful for trends; they are not a clinical sleep study. The iOS app does its own sleep analysis on your phone.

## Where data lives

Settings and state are in `sleepypod.db`. Sensor-derived measurements are in `biometrics.db`. Raw frames pass through an in-memory folder and may be archived to storage. See [architecture](/developers/architecture/).
