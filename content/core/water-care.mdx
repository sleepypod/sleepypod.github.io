# Water care and maintenance liquid

**BZK means benzalkonium chloride.** This is the ingredient named in sleepypod’s iOS **Water Care** panel. Eight Sleep also identifies it as an active ingredient in its Pod maintenance solution. The ingredient name alone does not specify a suitable liquid product, concentration, or dose.

## Find it in the iOS app

Open **Status**, then tap the **water-level indicator** in the health card. This opens **Water & Priming**; scroll to **Water Care** to find **Benzalkonium Chloride (BZK)**. Availability and layout depend on your installed build.

The app describes BZK as an antimicrobial and mentions adding liquid when refilling. It does **not** specify a product formulation, concentration, measured amount, or dosing interval. Treat that text as background information, not a mixing recipe.

## Choose care by hardware

The maintenance insert is part of the **cover’s water cord**. Check the hardware you actually have, especially if your hub and cover are from different generations.

| Hardware                                         | Maintenance guidance                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pod 3 or earlier with its original cover         | Eight Sleep’s current insert page says older Pods do not support the interchangeable insert. It does not provide a liquid BZK replacement dose for these models. Follow instructions specific to your hardware and maintenance product. |
| Pod 4 or Pod 5 cover with its maintenance insert | Eight Sleep specifies replacement every **6–8 months** and says no additional liquid other than water is needed in the tank. Follow the insert’s replacement instructions.                                                              |
| Mixed-generation hub and cover                   | The insert guidance does not establish a maintenance regimen for every mixed pairing. Confirm compatibility for the actual cover, cord, and hub rather than choosing by the hub label alone.                                            |

Source: [Eight Sleep’s Pod Maintenance Insert guidance](https://www.eightsleep.com/product/pod-maintenance-insert), checked October 26, 2026.

## Refilling and priming

Eight Sleep’s current refill guide specifies **distilled water for Pod 5 and earlier**. Pod 6 has different filling instructions, so do not transfer those instructions to an older hub. Follow your hardware’s fill-line instructions rather than estimating from the nominal water volume.

Priming circulates water through the cover and clears trapped air after refilling. In Core, [Settings → Device → Daily prime](/core/settings/device/#daily-maintenance) schedules that circulation. Priming and water treatment serve different purposes; enabling daily prime does not supply or measure a maintenance chemical.

Source: [Eight Sleep’s water reservoir and priming guide](https://help.eightsleep.com/en_us/how-often-will-i-need-to-refill-the-water-reservoir-H1EpdK0Uv), checked October 26, 2026.

## How much liquid BZK should I add?

The pinned sleepypod app source and manufacturer pages linked here do not establish a liquid BZK dose for an older Pod or a mixed-generation setup. A generic BZK product is not automatically equivalent to the manufacturer’s maintenance solution. Use a product and dose explicitly specified for your hardware; do not estimate an amount from the app’s wording.

<Callout type="info">

The app also flags hydrogen peroxide, bleach, and dehumidifier chemicals. Those labels are not a model-specific chemical compatibility specification. Use the current care instructions for your exact hardware and product rather than treating the app panel as a universal additive guide.

</Callout>
