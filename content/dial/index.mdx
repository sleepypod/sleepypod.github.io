# M5 Rotary Dial

A small bedside controller for the M5Stack Dial. Turn to adjust your side of the bed, click to turn it off, and let the screen fade into the background.

## Five simple controls

| Action               | Result                                                      |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Turn                 | Change target by 1° per detent, or 2° when turning quickly  |
| Click or tap power   | Turn the selected side off, or back on at its last setpoint |
| Hold or tap the gear | Open settings                                               |
| Settings → Side      | Pick and remember left or right                             |
| Touch while dim      | Wake the display; the next turn changes the target          |
