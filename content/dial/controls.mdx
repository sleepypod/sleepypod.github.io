# Controls and night mode

## Read the arc

The solid part represents the mattress temperature. The dim span toward the target shows the gap remaining. A shimmer communicates progress while the mattress is catching up.

<Demo src="/media/dial-loader.mp4" poster="/media/dial-heating.png" title="Getting there">
  The progress animation runs between the current temperature and the target. Silent device capture.
</Demo>

## Turn and click

A normal detent changes the target by one degree; faster rotation accelerates to two degrees. Two detents below the 55°F lower limit reach the off stop. A click or tap on the power icon turns the side off, then back on at the last setpoint.

<Demo src="/media/dial-power.mp4" poster="/media/dial-off.png" title="Off and back on">
  The arc empties when power is off and returns when the selected side is powered again. Silent
  device capture.
</Demo>

## Hold for settings

A ring fills during a hold. Releasing early cancels. Settings include Wi-Fi, manual Pod IP, discovery, temperature units, night mode, and side. A local temperature-unit choice is retained even when the Pod uses another display unit.

## At night

Automatic night mode runs from **22:00 to 07:00** in the configured timezone. It switches to red on black and disables sounds. You can force it on or off in settings.

The display dims after ten seconds of inactivity. Touching or turning while dim wakes it; make the next deliberate input to adjust the target.

## If the display looks wrong

Check night mode, timezone offsets, and whether the display is simply idle-dimmed. For connectivity and stale values, check the Pod IP and serial monitor. Normal synchronization occurs every 30 seconds.
